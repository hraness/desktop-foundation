# screen-recording · requester: local app
## cli pre-prompt, interactive
🔐 macOS will ask to let Example record your screen.
   Example only captures the window you pick. Change this any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording.
   Press Enter to continue · s to skip
